# 📸 The Vintage Booth

> **Capture the moment in timeless monochrome.**

A nostalgic, browser-based photo booth experience designed to turn ordinary moments into vintage-style keepsakes. **The Vintage Booth** lets users customize their photo appearance, choose a layout, capture their moments, and download the final photo — all directly from the browser.

🔗 **Live Demo:** [The Vintage Booth](https://yourveryownphotobooth.netlify.app/)

---

##  Features

###  Customize Your Look

Personalize your photos before entering the booth:

*  Choose your **frame color**
*  Select a **photo filter**
*  Choose a **frame pattern**
*  Create a unique vintage aesthetic

###  Multiple Photo Formats

Choose the format that fits your moment:

* **Photo Strip** — Vertical photo strip layout
* **Card** — 2×2 grid layout
* **Solo** — Single-photo layout

###  Flexible Photo Sessions

Choose the number of photos you want to capture and create your own personalized photo session.

###  Download Your Keepsake

Once your session is complete, generate your final photo and download it directly to your device.

###  Start a New Session

Finished your photos? Start a fresh session with a single click.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

This project was built as a modern frontend web experience using web technologies for interactive UI, camera/photo capture, customization, and client-side photo generation.

### Core Technologies

* **HTML5**
* **CSS3**
* **JavaScript**
* **Web APIs**
* **Canvas API**
* **Browser Camera APIs**

> The project is designed to run directly in the browser without requiring users to install a separate application.

##  What Makes It Different?

Instead of treating a photo booth as just a camera and capture button, **The Vintage Booth** focuses on the entire experience.

From choosing the visual style to selecting the layout and finally downloading the keepsake, every step is designed to feel like using a real vintage photo booth.

> **Your moment. Your style. Your keepsake.**
